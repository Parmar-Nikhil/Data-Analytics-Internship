# Project 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Objective
Analyze the e-commerce dataset to understand patterns, trends, distributions, and key business observations.

## Dataset
- Rows: 1,200
- Columns: 14
- Date range: 2023-01-01 to 2025-06-30

## Key KPIs
- Total recorded order value: ₹1,264,761.96
- Total orders: 1,200
- Unique customers: 1,189
- Total quantity sold: 3,535
- Average order value: ₹1,053.97
- Median order value: ₹823.62

## EDA Performed
1. Descriptive statistics
2. Monthly and yearly trend analysis
3. Product performance analysis
4. Payment method analysis
5. Order status analysis
6. Referral source analysis
7. Coupon analysis
8. Distribution analysis
9. IQR-based outlier detection
10. Key business insights

## Key Findings
- Chair is the top product by recorded sales.
- Instagram is the top referral source by recorded sales.
- Credit Card is the top payment method by recorded sales.
- 2024-06 has the highest order count.
- 2024-06 has the highest recorded sales.
- Cancelled and returned orders require business rules before calculating net revenue.
